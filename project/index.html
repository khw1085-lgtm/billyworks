About me

I’m Billy, a senior product designer who cares about making digital products feel clear, useful, and intuitive. I’m especially drawn to projects with the potential to make a positive impact.

My approach to design is grounded in curiosity, research, and collaboration. I work to understand what people need, then shape those insights into thoughtful experiences.

From early ideas to refined interfaces, I bring together product thinking and visual craft to create work that feels simple, considered, and effective.
